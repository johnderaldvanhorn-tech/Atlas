# ATLAS v8.4.2

## Fiscal Quarter Drill-Down

- Quarter View buttons now open a complete list of every record counted in the donut.
- Results are grouped into NPD, CI, Skunkworks, Cloud Sustained, and Edge Sustained.
- Product Initiative rows open the Product Initiative editor.
- Sustained rows open the Development Support editor.
- The drill-down uses the exact same quarter overlap and category rules as the dashboard counts.
- No Supabase migration is required.
